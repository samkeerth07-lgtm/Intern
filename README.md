🔍 Intern-Verify

AI-Powered Internship Trust Intelligence System

Intern-Verify is an AI-powered platform designed to help students and job seekers evaluate the trustworthiness, transparency, and authenticity of internship opportunities through evidence-based analysis.

Rather than simply labeling opportunities as "real" or "fake," the platform generates a comprehensive Trust Intelligence Report by combining organization verification, reputation analysis, internship authenticity checks, and explainable AI insights.

---

🚀 Problem Statement

Students often encounter internship opportunities from unfamiliar organizations and have limited ways to verify their legitimacy.

Traditional verification requires checking multiple sources such as company websites, professional networks, internship platforms, reviews, and online discussions. This process is time-consuming, inconsistent, and difficult to scale.

Intern-Verify simplifies this process by aggregating trust signals from multiple sources and presenting a transparent, evidence-backed assessment.

---

✨ Features

🎯 Trust Score

Generate an evidence-based trust score ranging from 0–100.

Score Range| Category
90–100| Excellent Trust
75–89| Good Trust
60–74| Moderate Trust
40–59| Low Trust
0–39| Critical Risk

🏢 Organization Intelligence

Analyze:

- Company existence
- Official website presence
- Domain age and credibility
- Contact information legitimacy
- Public visibility
- Social media activity
- Digital footprint

📋 Internship Authenticity Analysis

Verify:

- Internship listing consistency
- Recruiter legitimacy
- Historical internship activity
- Presence on trusted platforms
- Student testimonials
- Community discussions

⭐ Reputation Intelligence

Collect and summarize:

- Student experiences
- Public reviews
- Professional feedback
- Discussion forum sentiment
- Industry reputation

⚠️ Risk Detection

Identify:

- Missing company information
- Suspicious website behavior
- Unverified recruiters
- Unrealistic promises
- Contradictory information
- Weak digital presence

✅ Positive Indicators

Highlight:

- Verified organization presence
- Active employee profiles
- Consistent internship listings
- Positive community feedback
- Strong online reputation

🤖 Explainable AI Verdict

Possible outcomes include:

- Highly Trustworthy
- Likely Trustworthy
- Proceed with Verification
- Significant Concerns Detected
- High Risk Opportunity

---

📊 Trust Intelligence Report

The platform generates a professional report containing:

- Trust Score
- Confidence Level
- Executive Summary
- Positive Indicators
- Risk Indicators
- Evidence Sources
- Score Breakdown
- Recommended Actions

Every conclusion is accompanied by supporting evidence and explainable reasoning.
